# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how the developer of Reelier ("the app") handles information about people who use the app.

## 1. Information we collect

The app does not send any information to the developer or to third-party servers. There is no account sign-up. The app contains no advertising or analytics.

## 2. What is stored on your device

Shots (3-second videos) you take, Reelogs you create, and your settings are stored only on your device. The developer never sees or receives them.

If you uninstall the app, the shots and Reelogs stored in the app are removed from your device. Videos you saved to your camera roll remain.

## 3. Permissions and why we use them

- Camera: to take shots.
- Microphone: to record sound with shots. If you turn off "Record sound" in Settings, no sound is recorded.
- Photos and videos (camera roll): to save videos to the "Movies/Reelier" folder on your device when you export a Reelog, and when "Save each shot to camera roll" is on. On Android 10 and later, no special permission is needed for this.

The app does not access your location, contacts, or any other information.

## 4. Sharing

When you tap Share, the exported video is passed to the Android share sheet. Once the video is sent to the app you choose (such as Instagram), that app's terms and privacy policy apply.

## 5. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce it in the app and on the store page.
